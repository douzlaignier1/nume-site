About Nume (English)

Nume builds AI customer service for small businesses in Brazil that sell through WhatsApp. We connect to the official WhatsApp Business API as a Meta Tech Provider, import up to six months of a client's conversation history, deliver a diagnostic report of their real service data, and then deploy an AI agent trained on their own conversations that answers leads, books appointments in the client's CRM and hands off to a human when needed. Our first clients are a network of dance schools in Belo Horizonte.
